— Data —

Weather forecast: Data from MET Norway (https://api.met.no), under CC BY 4.0. On the Pro plan: Powered by WeatherAPI.com
  (https://www.weatherapi.com).
Airport observation (METAR): Aviation Weather Center, NOAA (US public domain).
Cities and time zones: GeoNames (https://www.geonames.org), under CC BY 4.0. The city search runs on the TV.
Movie and series details, posters, cast, age ratings and ratings: Trakt (https://trakt.tv).
  Sofaly uses Trakt but is not endorsed or certified by Trakt. Community rating: Simkl (https://simkl.com), with the
  person's own account.
Subtitles: Subtitles provided by OpenSubtitles.com (https://www.opensubtitles.com), with the person's own account.
  Each subtitle stays only on the TV that asked for it.
Episode intros (skip intro): IntroDB (https://introdb.app), a community database.
Background artworks: The Metropolitan Museum of Art, Open Access (CC0).
Radio stations: Radio Browser (https://www.radio-browser.info).
Address from the Brazilian postal code (CEP): ViaCEP (https://viacep.com.br).
Real sky and screensaver: GOES satellite clouds (NOAA, US public domain) through NASA GIBS
  (https://earthdata.nasa.gov/gibs); Natural Earth map (https://www.naturalearthdata.com, public domain);
  Space Station orbit (NORAD TLE) through CelesTrak (https://celestrak.org).
"Earth seen from space" screensaver: photos taken by the Space Station astronauts, NASA (https://images.nasa.gov;
  no copyright in the US, credit to NASA; NASA does not support or endorse this app).
