Extend Sofaly from your own app

A plugin is your own Android TV app. There are four kinds, and one app can be several of them:

KindWhat it doesHow
CardA short card (title, big value, a line of detail, a color, optionally a picture) in the row of the world you choose: Watch, Listen, Play or Apps.Provider with tv.sofaly.plugin.CARTAO, below.
Quick panel buttonA button in the quick panel (hold Back) that runs an action of your app: a lamp, a VPN, a scene. It can also be a step of a scene or a routine.Provider with tv.sofaly.plugin.TILE, below.
WallpaperThe home screen background, changing with the time and with what has focus. Existing wallpaper-provider plugins for Android TV launchers work as they are.
Icon packReplaces app icons and banners.Any pack with an appfilter.xml.
WidgetYour widget, live, on the home screen (up to 3). People place and resize it with the remote, within your min/max size.A normal Android AppWidgetProvider.

You never draw on the home screen, and the launcher never sends you anything about the person's apps, accounts or channels (a wallpaper only hears the title and type of what has focus).

The plugin template has two ready apps you can build and install today: a countdown to a date the person picks on the TV, and today's moon phase. No internet, no account. MIT No Attribution: copy it, rename the package, publish.

Card

1. Declare a provider

<provider
    android:name=".CardProvider"
    android:authorities="com.example.score.card"
    android:exported="true">
    <intent-filter>
        <action android:name="tv.sofaly.plugin.CARTAO" />
    </intent-filter>
</provider>

2. Answer content://<authority>/cartao with one row

ColumnLimitMeaning
titulo30 charsCard title, e.g. "Match score".
valor12 charsThe big value, e.g. "2 x 1", "18°", "$5.42".
detalhe80 charsShown at the top of the screen when the card has focus.
cor#RRGGBBBackground color. Transparency is ignored.
acao500 chars, optionalAn intent: URI opened on OK. It must stay inside your own package: no component=, no selector, no other package=. Without it, OK opens your app.
worldoptionalwatch (default), listen, play or apps: the row the card joins.
image500 chars, optionalA content:// URI of a provider in your own package. Shown up to 256 px, read with the same 2-second limit. Anything else is ignored and the color is used.
class CardProvider : ContentProvider() {
    override fun onCreate() = true
    override fun query(uri: Uri, p: Array<String>?, s: String?, a: Array<String>?, o: String?): Cursor =
        MatrixCursor(arrayOf("titulo", "valor", "detalhe", "cor", "acao")).apply {
            addRow(arrayOf("Match score", "2 x 1", "Second half, 40 min", "#1E8E3E", null))
        }
    // insert/update/delete/getType: return null or 0
}

Control characters and extra spaces are removed, and longer text is cut. A row with neither a title nor a value is ignored.

Quick panel button

<provider android:name=".TileProvider" android:authorities="com.example.lamp.tile" android:exported="true">
    <intent-filter><action android:name="tv.sofaly.plugin.TILE" /></intent-filter>
</provider>

query() on content://<authority>/tile returns one row; on OK the launcher calls call(uri, "click", null, null), you do your action, and the row is read again to repaint the button.

ColumnLimitMeaning
title14 charsButton label. A row without it is ignored.
detail16 chars, optionalSecond line, e.g. "Connected".
stateoptionalon, off or nothing (a button without a state).
iconoptionalOne of: light, plug, fan, curtain, scene, routine, key, ac, thermometer, camera, door, home, dns, audio, timer, filter, wifi, bluetooth, screensaver, sleep. The launcher draws it; any other value gets its default icon.

At most 2 buttons are shown. A scene (in the TV web panel) or a routine can press your button too, with the step "Button from another app".

What the person sees

  • Nothing appears until the person turns your plugin on in Settings › Appearance › Plugins, which asks for the parental PIN.
  • The OK is tied to your signing certificate. An update signed with another key is turned off until the person allows it again.
  • At most 3 cards and 2 buttons are shown. Each read has a 2-second limit, done away from the screen thread: a slow plugin is skipped, never waited for.
  • A plugin that fails 5 times in a row within 6 hours, or keeps taking more than 2.5 seconds, is turned off by itself. The person sees why in Plugins and can turn it on again.
  • Cards, buttons, widgets, wallpapers and icon packs from other developers are part of the Pro plan. Turning one off is always free.
  • The card is read again every time the person returns to the home screen. Keep query fast and local (cache your data).

Get listed

Plugins on the Play Store can be listed in the showcase every Sofaly TV shows. Fill in Submit a plugin: a bot checks the rules and the Play Store in a minute and answers on the issue; a person only looks when the bot is not sure. A listed plugin that later freezes, shows ads over the launcher or does what it didn't say is turned off on every TV.
